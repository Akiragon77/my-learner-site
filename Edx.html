About edX

What is edX? Platform Overview

edX is a world-renowned online learning platform founded in 2012 by scientists from Harvard University and MIT (Massachusetts Institute of Technology). Now part of 2U, Inc., edX connects over 80 million learners worldwide with top-tier university education and industry-led training programs.

Key Features of edX

  • Elite Academic Leadership: Built by Ivy League universities, edX offers courses from world-class institutions including Harvard, MIT, Berkeley, and Oxford.
  • Free "Audit" Access: Most courses allow students to access lecture videos and reading materials for free through the free audit track.
  • MicroBachelors & MicroMasters Programs: Unique credential programs designed to bridge the gap between higher education and advanced career skills with potential college credit pathways.
  • Industry Partnerships: Programs developed in collaboration with top global tech leaders like Microsoft, IBM, and Google.
