How to Sign Up on edX

1. How to Create a Free edX Account

Joining edX is completely free and takes less than two minutes. Follow these simple steps to set up your account:

  1. Visit the Official Website: Go to edX.org or download the official edX mobile app for iOS or Android.
  2. Click "Register": Click the prominent red or dark Register button located at the top-right corner of the homepage.
  3. Fill in Your Profile Details: Enter your full name, public username, email address, country, and a password. You can also sign up instantly using your Google, Apple, Facebook, or Microsoft account.
  4. Verify Your Email: Check your inbox for an activation email from edX and click the verification link to complete registration.

2. How to Enroll in Courses (Free Audit vs. Verified Track)

When enrolling in a course on edX, you can choose between two main tracks depending on your learning goals:

Understanding Enrollment Tracks

  • Free Audit Track (Free Course Access): Choose this option to access lecture videos, course readings, and discussion forums for free during the course session. It is ideal if you just want to learn without needing a certificate.
  • Verified Track (Paid Certificate): Pay a course fee to gain unlimited access to course materials, graded assignments, exams, and an official verified certificate signed by the university or institution upon completion.
Need Assistance? edX offers a generous Financial Assistance Program that provides up to 90% discount on verified certificates for learners who demonstrate financial need. You can apply directly through your edX account dashboard.
