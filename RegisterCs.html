How to Sign Up on Coursera

1. How to Create a Free Coursera Account

Signing up for Coursera is completely free and grants you access to thousands of course materials, free audits, and preview lectures. Follow these steps to get started:

  1. Visit the Official Website: Go to Coursera.org on your computer, or download the official Coursera app from the iOS App Store or Google Play Store.
  2. Click "Join for Free": Click the prominent blue Join for Free button at the top-right corner of the page.
  3. Choose Registration Method: Enter your full name, email address, and a secure password. Alternatively, choose quick registration via Google, Facebook, or Apple ID.
  4. Complete Profile setup: Indicate your current career status or learning goals so Coursera can recommend tailored courses to you.

2. How to Enroll in Courses & Select Payment Options

Coursera offers flexible ways to take courses based on whether you need an official certificate or just want to learn for free:

Enrollment Options

  • Free Audit (Free Course Content): When clicking "Enroll," look for the small "Audit the course" option at the bottom of the pop-up. This lets you watch videos and access reading materials without paying a fee (certificates/graded quizzes not included).
  • Individual Course Purchase: Pay a one-time fee for a single course to gain full access to graded assignments, peer reviews, and an official university/industry certificate.
  • 7-Day Free Trial (Specializations & Professional Certificates): Most career programs offer a 7-day free trial. After the trial, it transitions into a monthly subscription (cancel anytime).
  • Coursera Plus Subscription: If you plan to earn multiple certificates throughout the year, Coursera Plus offers unlimited access for a monthly or annual fee.
Financial Aid Option: If you cannot afford the course or certificate fee, Coursera offers a Financial Aid / Scholarship program. You can submit an application explaining your financial situation and career goals directly from the course page.
