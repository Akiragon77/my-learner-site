About Udemy

What is Udemy? Platform Overview

Udemy is one of the world's leading online learning platforms, offering over 200,000 video courses across a wide range of subjects. Founded in 2010, it connects millions of students globally with expert instructors, allowing anyone to learn new skills or upgrade their professional career at their own pace.

Key Features of Udemy

  • Vast Selection of Topics: Offers comprehensive courses in programming, AI, business, web design, marketing, music, and personal development.
  • Lifetime Access: Once you purchase a course, you get full lifetime access to the video lectures, downloadable resources, and all future content updates.
  • Learn Anytime, Anywhere: Access lectures on desktop or via mobile apps (iOS/Android), including offline viewing capabilities.
  • Direct Instructor Q&A: Ask questions directly to course instructors through the built-in Q&A platform for guidance and troubleshooting.
