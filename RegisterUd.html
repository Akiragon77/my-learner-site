How to Sign Up on Udemy

Step-by-Step Guide to Creating an Account

Creating an account on Udemy is completely free and takes less than two minutes. Follow these simple steps to get started:

  1. Visit the Official Website: Head over to Udemy.com on your desktop browser or download the Udemy mobile app on iOS/Android.
  2. Click "Sign Up": Locate and click the Sign Up button situated at the top-right corner of the homepage.
  3. Enter Your Details: Fill in your full name, email address, and a secure password. Alternatively, you can choose single sign-on options via Google, Apple, or Facebook for faster access.
  4. Verify Your Email: Check your inbox for a confirmation email from Udemy and click the verification link to activate your account.

How to Buy Your First Course

Once your account is created, enrolling in a course is seamless and secure:

  1. Search for a Topic: Type keywords (e.g., "Python", "React", "ChatGPT") into the search bar at the top of the page.
  2. Select a Course: Click on a course to view its landing page, instructor profile, ratings, syllabus, and free video previews.
  3. Add to Cart or Buy Now: Click Buy Now for immediate checkout or Add to Cart if you wish to purchase multiple courses together.
  4. Complete Payment: Select your preferred payment method. Udemy accepts major credit/debit cards, PayPal, and Apple Pay / Google Pay.
30-Day Money-Back Guarantee: Every paid course on Udemy comes with a 30-day refund policy. If a course doesn't meet your expectations, you can request a full refund hassle-free within 30 days of purchase.
